Revenue by region

Q3 FY26, sorted by revenue (descending)

View SQL 
SELECT region, SUM(revenue_usd) AS revenue, COUNT(*) AS orders
FROM HTML_REPORTS_QS.DEMO.SALES
WHERE order_date BETWEEN '2026-07-01' AND '2026-09-30'
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY revenue DESC

Revenue share by segment

Q3 FY26, share of total revenue

View SQL 
SELECT segment, SUM(revenue_usd) AS revenue, RATIO_TO_REPORT(SUM(revenue_usd)) OVER () AS share
FROM HTML_REPORTS_QS.DEMO.SALES
WHERE order_date BETWEEN '2026-07-01' AND '2026-09-30'
GROUP BY segment
ORDER BY revenue DESC
