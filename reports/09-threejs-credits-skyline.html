SELECT DAY_NAME,
       MIN(HOUR_START)::DATE AS DAY_DATE,
       COUNT(*) AS N,
       LISTAGG(TO_VARCHAR(CREDITS, 'FM90.00'), ',')
         WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY HOUR_OF_DAY) AS CREDITS_BY_HOUR,
       SUM(CREDITS) AS DAY_TOTAL
FROM HTML_REPORTS_QS.DEMO.WAREHOUSE_CREDITS_HOURLY
GROUP BY DAY_NAME
ORDER BY DAY_DATE

What this shows: Credits consumed in each of the 168 hours of one week. Columns run left to right from 00:00 to 23:00; rows run back to front from Monday to Sunday.

Source: HTML_REPORTS_QS.DEMO.WAREHOUSE_CREDITS_HOURLY, all 168 rows (HOUR_START 2026-11-09 00:00 through 2026-11-15 23:00), no filters.

How it was derived: One bar per row. Height is 1 scene unit per credit. Color is interpolated blue → teal → yellow → red between the week's lowest hour (0.84, Fri 00:00) and highest (6.00, Wed 09:00 and Fri 08:00).

Pattern: Weekday business hours (08:00–18:59) never drop below 4.02 credits per hour, and usage falls sharply at 19:00. Weekends stay at or below 2.80 credits per hour all day.

Caveat: This is a single week of demo data, so it doesn't show week-to-week variation. The warehouse(s) behind the totals aren't broken out in the table.
