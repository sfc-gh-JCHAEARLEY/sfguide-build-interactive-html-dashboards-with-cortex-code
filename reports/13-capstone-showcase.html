SELECT region,
       ROUND(SUM(revenue_usd), 2) AS revenue,
       COUNT(*)                   AS orders
FROM HTML_REPORTS_QS.DEMO.SALES
WHERE order_date BETWEEN '2026-07-01' AND '2026-09-30'
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY revenue DESC

Every 5th row in a stable sort order, excluding the 5 rows with duration_ms = 0 (they can't sit on a log axis):

SELECT warehouse_size, query_type, rows_scanned, duration_ms
FROM HTML_REPORTS_QS.DEMO.QUERY_LOG
WHERE duration_ms > 0
QUALIFY MOD(ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
          ORDER BY rows_scanned, duration_ms, warehouse_size, query_type), 5) = 0

Tips

Before you share

Reports are snapshots

Data is embedded when the report is built and doesn't re-query when someone opens it. To refresh, rerun the queries (or use Refresh report in Workspaces), then publish again. The link stays the same.

Use the approved libraries

Scripts load only from the vendored /libs/ path: Chart.js, Plotly (cartesian), D3, Vega, Mermaid, KaTeX, highlight.js, Marked, and Three.js. No CDNs, fetch, iframes, cookies, or local storage.

Design for every screen and theme

Reports open on phones, in narrow side panels, and in dark mode. Ask for grids that stack on narrow screens, fixed-height chart boxes, and colors that adapt to light and dark.
